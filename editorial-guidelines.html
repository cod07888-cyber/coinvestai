Editorial Guidelines & Standards

Updated: August 13, 2026

Core Mission: CoinvestAI is dedicated to publishing rigorous, transparent, and independent research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, quantitative finance, cryptocurrency markets, and digital asset analytics.

1. Editorial Independence & Objectivity

CoinvestAI maintains absolute separation between editorial judgment and commercial considerations. Our analysts, researchers, and writers operate independently of advertisers, affiliate partners, and third-party software developers.

No commercial relationship or advertising arrangement can secure a favorable product rating, bypass technical evaluation, or alter research conclusions published on our site.

Advertisement

2. Fact-Checking & Technical Verification

Financial technology content demands exceptional technical accuracy. Every publication undergoes a multi-layer verification workflow before release:

  • Primary Source Citation: Claims regarding AI model architectures, smart contract exploits, or financial data must reference official documentation, whitepapers, SEC filings, or verifiable on-chain data telemetry.
  • Hands-On Testing: Software reviews (e.g., Liquid Co-Invest or Manus AI) incorporate practical testing within live or sandboxed execution environments.
  • Peer Review: Technical guides undergo internal review by quantitative research staff prior to publication.

3. Responsible AI & Generative AI Policy

While CoinvestAI reports on artificial intelligence, we adhere to strict standards regarding AI usage in content creation:

  • Human Oversight: All articles are written, reviewed, and approved by qualified human editors and subject-matter analysts.
  • No Synthetic Hallucinations: AI assistance tools used during research must have all generated data points independently verified against primary financial records.
  • Algorithmic Transparency: When evaluating AI trading algorithms or LLMs, we explicitly highlight model limitations, halluncination risks, and execution latency.

4. Corrections & Updates Policy

Financial markets and software specifications evolve rapidly. When a factual error or outdated specification is identified, CoinvestAI updates the article promptly:

  • Minor Updates: Spelling or formatting adjustments are corrected silently.
  • Substantive Corrections: Significant updates or numerical adjustments are accompanied by an updated timestamp and a clear correction note detailing the revision.

Readers can submit correction reports or technical feedback directly via our Contact Page or by emailing editorial@coinvestai.com.

5. Advertising & Affiliate Transparency

To fund our independent research infrastructure, CoinvestAI participates in digital advertising programs (such as Google AdSense) and selective affiliate partnerships. All affiliate links are clearly distinguished, and full disclosures are available on our Disclaimer page.

6. Educational Boundaries & YMYL Compliance

Articles on CoinvestAI are prepared exclusively for educational and informational research. We do not provide personalized financial, legal, or investment advice, nor do we issue guaranteed market forecasts. For complete methodology details, please visit our Review Methodology.
