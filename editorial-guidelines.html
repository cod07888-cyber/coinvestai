Editorial Guidelines

At CoinvestAI, we are committed to delivering independent, accurate, and rigorous research on AI-driven financial tools, algorithmic trading, and fintech innovation.

1. Accuracy & Fact-Checking

Our editorial team verifies data, financial metrics, and technological claims before publication. We cross-reference metrics with primary industry sources, official documentation, and empirical backtests to ensure our insights reflect real-world functionality.

2. Independence & Integrity

Our research and reviews remain strictly independent. While we may utilize affiliate links or partner programs, commercial relationships never dictate our ratings, scoring methodologies, or analytical conclusions. An AI tool or platform earns its reputation strictly through performance, usability, and transparency.

3. Transparency in AI Usage

As a platform dedicated to AI and financial technology, we leverage advanced machine learning tools for data gathering, structural optimization, and market research. However, every article, review, and publication is rigorously overseen, reviewed, and finalized by human editors to maintain professional standards.

4. Corrections Policy

If financial data, statistics, or software details change, we update our publications promptly. Should an error occur, we correct it transparently with a clear note indicating the update to maintain full accountability with our readers.

5. Financial Disclaimer

CoinvestAI provides research, analytical insights, and educational content only. Nothing published on this platform constitutes formal financial, investment, or legal advice. Always conduct independent research or consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions.
