What Is Liquid Co-Invest?

Liquid Co-Invest is an AI-assisted trading and market-research service associated with the Liquid trading platform. Instead of requiring the user to conduct every research step inside a conventional trading interface, Co-Invest connects a Liquid account with supported AI environments such as Claude and ChatGPT.

How Co-Invest Works

The service is designed around a conversational workflow. Instead of filling out a traditional order ticket first, a user can begin with a natural-language research question. The AI can then retrieve supported market information, discuss the available context, and formulate a potential trade idea.

Key Features

  • Conversational market research: Users can ask questions about markets using natural language.
  • Market information: Access to price, funding, open interest, and positioning data.
  • Explicit confirmation: Standard Co-Invest trading actions require user confirmation before order submission.
  • Paper trading: Test strategies using virtual balances against live market data.

Pros & Cons
