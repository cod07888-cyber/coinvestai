Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CoinvestAI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, utilize, and protect your information when you access and browse coinvestai.com.

By using our website, you signify your acceptance of the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please discontinue using CoinvestAI.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information through two primary methods: direct voluntary submission and automated digital logging.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, or message content submitted via our contact forms or editorial inquiries.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Email address provided if you elect to subscribe to CoinvestAI research updates.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Log & Device Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, geographical country code, referring URLs, time stamps, and pages viewed.
  • Cookies & Analytics Data: Usage metrics collected via analytics providers to measure platform performance and reader engagement.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Delivering and maintaining the functionality of our educational articles and AI tools directory.
  • Analyzing site telemetry to optimize page speed, user interface design, and mobile responsiveness.
  • Responding directly to user inquiries, technical reports, or editorial feedback.
  • Serving contextual and personalized digital advertisements via network partners.

4. Cookies, Tracking & Google AdSense Compliance

CoinvestAI uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and serve advertisements. For detailed information on managing cookie preferences, please consult our Cookie Policy.

Google AdSense Disclosure: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to CoinvestAI or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads tailored to your browsing activity. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

5. Data Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & CPRA)

Depending on your legal jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you are entitled to key privacy rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of personal data stored by CoinvestAI.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of your contact information from our databases.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Direct us not to sell or share personal data (Note: CoinvestAI does not sell personal user data to third-party data brokers).

To exercise any of these privacy rights, please submit a request to our compliance officer at privacy@coinvestai.com.

6. Data Security & Third-Party Links

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access or alteration. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

Our website contains external links to third-party financial platforms, AI software providers, and reference databases. CoinvestAI is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated modification date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy or data security practices, please contact us via email at privacy@coinvestai.com or visit our Contact Page.
