TradingView

Charting & Technical Analysis Platform
CoinvestAI Rating: ★ 4.7/5 How We Rate
Updated: August 2026

TradingView is a widely-used charting platform that combines advanced technical analysis tools with AI-powered indicators, screening capabilities, and a social trading community. In 2026, it remains one of the most popular platforms for traders and investors.

Key Features

  • Advanced charting with 100+ built-in indicators
  • Pine Script for custom indicator development
  • AI-powered screening and alerts
  • Social trading community and idea sharing
  • Multi-asset coverage (stocks, crypto, forex, futures)
  • Paper trading for strategy testing

Detailed Review

TradingView excels at providing professional-grade charting tools accessible via web browser. The platform's AI-enhanced features include smart drawing tools, pattern recognition, and customizable alerts. Pine Script allows users to create and backtest custom strategies.

Pros & Cons

Pros: Excellent charting, intuitive interface, strong community, extensive asset coverage, free tier available.

Cons: Advanced features require paid plans, limited fundamental data on free tier, some AI features still evolving.
