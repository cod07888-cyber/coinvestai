Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit websites. They are widely utilized to make digital platforms function smoothly, improve browsing performance, provide personalized content, and furnish operational telemetry to website operators.

Advertisement

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

CoinvestAI uses distinct categories of first-party and third-party cookies across our website:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for underlying platform security, session integrity, page navigation, and basic feature execution.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Placed via Google Analytics to aggregate anonymous metrics on reader engagement, popular research topics, and page loading speeds.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies: Placed by third-party advertising networks, primarily Google AdSense, to serve relevant advertisements aligned with user interests and browsing telemetry across internet properties.
  • Preference Cookies: Store user choices such as search queries in our AI Tools Directory or category filter selections.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, utilize cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to CoinvestAI or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their browsing activity across digital properties.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by managing their preferences directly through Google Ads Settings or by visiting AboutAds.info.

Operational Notice: Disabling or clearing specific functional or performance cookies via your web browser settings may impact certain user interface features, such as filtering tools in our interactive directory.

4. How to Control & Opt-Out of Cookies

You maintain full authority to manage or disable cookie preferences directly within your internet browser software. Most modern web browsers allow you to inspect stored cookies, clear cached data, or block third-party cookies automatically:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may periodically update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our technological stack, regulatory requirements (such as GDPR or CCPA), or advertising partner frameworks. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated modification date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our use of cookies or privacy practices, please contact our editorial and compliance team via email at privacy@coinvestai.com or visit our Contact Page.
