About CoinvestAI

Independent research and quantitative analysis on how artificial intelligence is transforming modern finance and investment in 2026.

Our Mission & Vision

CoinvestAI was founded with a singular objective: to provide transparent, rigorous, and independent research on artificial intelligence technologies reshaping global financial markets, quantitative trading, digital asset intelligence, and corporate auditing.

As financial technology evolves at an unprecedented pace, market participants—from retail investors to institutional analysts—require unbiased evaluations of AI trading platforms, autonomous agents, predictive analytics models, and regulatory compliance frameworks.

Core Pillars of CoinvestAI

1. Uncompromising Independence

Our evaluations, software reviews, and technical guides are conducted independently without commercial bias or paid rankings.

2. Scientific & Quantitative Rigor

We test financial AI platforms against live telemetry, execution confirmation controls, and security risk frameworks.

3. Risk Transparency & Safety

We actively highlight market risks, model limitations, leverage dangers, and regulatory standards like the EU AI Act.

Editorial Independence & Integrity

Editorial Commitment: CoinvestAI maintains absolute separation between commercial disclosures and research conclusions. No provider can pay to alter rating scores, hide limitations, or influence review outcomes.
