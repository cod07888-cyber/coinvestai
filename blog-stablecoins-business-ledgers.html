Stablecoins Move From Trading Floors to Company Ledgers

August 15, 2026 · Fintech & Crypto AI

For most of their history, stablecoins were a trading tool. In 2026, that framing is out of date. A growing share of stablecoin activity is now genuine commercial payments — invoices, payroll, and treasury transfers moving through corporate finance departments.

The Regulatory "Safety" Floor

The GENIUS Act (2025) and MiCA regulation have established the operational boundaries for issuers. CFOs are now integrating these rails into accounting software because the legal risk has been mitigated by clear federal frameworks.
